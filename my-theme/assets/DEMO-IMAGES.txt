The two demo-*.jpg files are coffee, not equipment. Earlier versions of this
theme shipped stovetop moka-pot stock, which illustrated claims about a
different object; those have been removed. Do not reintroduce photography of
brewing equipment that is not this product.

Both product crops come from one supplied photo in which the machine occupies
only 195 x 599 px. They are cut at native resolution and never upscaled, which
is why the hero caps the image at 560px wide. Displaying them larger makes them
visibly soft. Real product photography is the fix.

Every section prefers a picked image over these files, so uploading real
photography in the theme editor replaces them with no code change.
